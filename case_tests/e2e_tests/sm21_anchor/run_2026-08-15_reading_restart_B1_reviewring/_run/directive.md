# Per-run directive

## 1. Measurement (unchanged from the 2026-07-07 run this directive is restored from)

cv_toolbox.md is REQUIRED reading for this run, and wall-line / window-box /
storey-line positions must be measured with `python tools/run_cv_probe.py`
before drawing (measure-before-draw).

## 2. Pilot-then-stop — this OVERRIDES session_kickoff.md for this run

`session_kickoff.md` currently says "Nobody reviews your work mid-run", "There
is no review point", and "If you find yourself about to stop and ask whether to
continue, that is the signal to keep going". **For this run those three
sentences do not apply.** This directive is binding and replaces them.

Instead:

1. Do **one** pilot image first: the first plan view.
2. Then **stop**. Do not start any other image. Write what you have and end
   your turn. A review of that pilot will be produced and handed back to you as
   `feedback.md` in a later session.
3. Do not ask a question in your final message — nobody reads it interactively.
   Just state plainly what you did on the pilot, what you measured it with, and
   what you are unsure about.

Completing only the pilot is the correct and expected outcome of this session.
Finishing the other images now would be a failure to follow this directive.
