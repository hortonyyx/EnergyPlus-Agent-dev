A drawing reconstruction method for the supplied originals.

Establish the building's perimeter, physical dividers and openings from the
whole drawing and suitable clean enlargements. Follow each divider through its
junctions and door symbols, and each space around its complete boundary.
Represent a drawn internal wall by its centre line when that matches the
dimensioned layout; identify the reference plane of exterior dimensions too.
Wall fill colour is a drawing convention. Use the complete linework, junctions,
door symbols and relevant dimensions together to decide whether a divider exists.

Use readable dimension labels with their actual extension endpoints to derive
the layout in a consistent frame. Check units and a second known span on each
axis. Use map_pixels/map_dimension_chain for arithmetic. Where labels do not
resolve a position, measure the relevant local feature with the available pixel
tools and state the chosen representative plane. A profile answers the selected
local pixel question; assess that question's relevance to the physical object.
All submitted pixel coordinates refer to the original image. Apply the crop's
reported transform when interpreting a cropped or resized view. Selected saved
measurements can be referenced directly in plan declarations when useful.

Keep stable door/window identities across plan and elevation. Use facade,
storey, order, complete span and adjacent features to match each opening, then
read its actual sill/head or applicable dimension chain. Check each distinct
height family before sharing values. Preserve exceptions, including small or
tall windows. State assumptions for properties absent from the supplied views.

Build the physical paths and apertures with the existing deterministic tools.
Inspect the saved source plan, original overlay and relevant source elevations.
Compare actual dividers, spaces on both sides, complete opening spans and height
families to the originals. On a conflict, reobserve the affected original object,
revise the supported property locally, and inspect the saved change and affected
hosts/connections. Keep unrelated declarations intact. A self-consistent model
or confirmation of a saved value does not establish its correspondence to the
drawing. Finish with the actual remaining uncertainties and completed scope.
