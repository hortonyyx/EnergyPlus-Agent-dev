模型采用的假设
  • Correction cell rings represent physical source spaces with horizontal floor/ceiling boundaries.
  • Contacts describe adjacency, not traversability or EnergyPlus surface pairing.
  • Only explicit openings are represented; absent observations do not establish a solid wall.
  • Source boundary identity follows cell identity and ring order; topology edits need identity migration.
  • Wall representative plane = measured pixel line centrelines/outer faces from pixel_profile scans, not nominal 240mm offsets.
  • Window/door heights: North window sill1.0/head3.4m, South windows sill1.0/head2.8m (directly measured). East/West window heights assumed equal to North pattern (sill1.0/head3.4m).
  • All doors assumed 2.4m height, base 0 (floor level).
  • Building height 4.5m used uniformly for the single floor per all four elevations.
  • The corridor (north-south spine) and the south-east furniture area merge into ONE physical room: no full-height wall separates them in the pixel evidence (a short non-connecting wall fragment near x440-560,y698 was omitted since it cannot close a separate region).
  • Room roles are inferred from furniture symbols, not labelled in the source.
  • East wall opening x-positions were derived from East_view.png's own dimension chain, not confirmed by explicit marks on the plan's east wall face; two openings (ED1, EW1) were clipped to fit plan-measured room boundaries since their elevation-derived spans crossed those boundaries.
  • Plan spaces were compiled deterministically from the declared footprint and physical partition segments; no snapping, extension, clipping, or inferred partition was applied.
