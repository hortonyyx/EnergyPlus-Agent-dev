模型采用的假设
  • Correction cell rings represent physical source spaces with horizontal floor/ceiling boundaries.
  • Contacts describe adjacency, not traversability or EnergyPlus surface pairing.
  • Only explicit openings are represented; absent observations do not establish a solid wall.
  • Source boundary identity follows cell identity and ring order; topology edits need identity migration.
  • Exterior wall/floor-to-ceiling height 4500mm from elevation chains.
  • Window sill 1000mm, head 3400mm (2400mm tall) assumed uniformly from elevation window chain 1100/2400/1000.
  • Door heights 0-2400mm assumed uniformly (interior and exterior) from elevation door chain pattern.
  • Vestibule area near the east exterior door merged into south_east_room as one physical room rather than a separate small jog, since wall evidence there was a minor step not a full room division.
  • North room's east-wall window (O6) position estimated from East_view's upper 1500mm segment; exact plan pixel hatch boundary approximate.
  • South_east_room's east-wall small windows (O9) approximated as one combined opening representing East_view's two small window rectangles near the south end.
  • Plan spaces were compiled deterministically from the declared footprint and physical partition segments; no snapping, extension, clipping, or inferred partition was applied.
