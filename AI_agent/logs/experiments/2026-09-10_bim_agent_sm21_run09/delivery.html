模型采用的假设
  • Correction cell rings represent physical source spaces with horizontal floor/ceiling boundaries.
  • Contacts describe adjacency, not traversability or EnergyPlus surface pairing.
  • Only explicit openings are represented; absent observations do not establish a solid wall.
  • Source boundary identity follows cell identity and ring order; topology edits need identity migration.
  • Plan top edge = North, bottom edge = South, left = West, right = East, confirmed by matching South elevation entrance door and West elevation corridor door positions to plan.
  • Wall thickness ~0.24m per plan annotations; modeled as zero-thickness representative planes at wall centerlines.
  • F1 ceiling height 3.0m, F2 3.6m, per East/West elevation vertical dimension chains.
  • Window sill/head heights read from elevation dimension chains: F1 z=[1.0,2.6], F2 z=[4.0,5.8].
  • Door heights: 2.4m for the two main glazed exterior doors (South main entrance, West corridor entrance) per elevation proportions; 2.1m for all other doors including the secondary south door D_R1S_ext2 and interior room-to-corridor doors.
  • East facade has a window (not door) into the corridor on both floors; West facade has a door into the corridor on F1 and a window in the same position on F2 - confirmed via East_view.png/West_view.png.
  • F1_R1S has TWO exterior south doors: D_main (main glazed entrance) and D_R1S_ext2 (secondary door near SW corner, width 900mm per plan dimension chain 540/900). D_R1S_ext2 is supported by direct plan evidence (arc + dimension chain; geometry re-verified by overlay_candidate, which projects the modeled door exactly onto this plan arc) but is NOT corroborated by South_view.png, which shows a plain, ungapped wall across that span (re-checked directly, no second door glyph present). The seed proposal's prior claim of a matching South_view.png glyph could not be confirmed on re-inspection and is retracted; D_R1S_ext2 is retained on plan evidence alone.
  • Interior room-to-corridor door widths/positions are approximated (~0.75-1m) from arc marks; F2 office door jamb offsets are approximated/centered since no explicit dimension callout exists for them in 2f_view.png (the nearby dimension chain there measures the south-facade window positions, not the corridor doors).
  • Overlay calibration (both F1 on 1f_view.png and F2 on 2f_view.png) shows a ~1.5-2% cross-axis (x vs y) pixel/metre scale disagreement when independently reading the '15000' and '8000' dimension-line tick marks from freshly re-measured pixel positions. This was investigated: the overlay projection nonetheless aligns tightly with all wall, door-arc and window-hatch positions in both drawings (visually confirmed), so the discrepancy is attributed to sub-10px tick-reading imprecision at this image resolution rather than a modeled geometry error. The blended scale was not trusted uncritically; alignment was checked visually against the source raster instead.
