模型采用的假设
  • Correction cell rings represent physical source spaces with horizontal floor/ceiling boundaries.
  • Contacts describe adjacency, not traversability or EnergyPlus surface pairing.
  • Only explicit openings are represented; absent observations do not establish a solid wall.
  • Source boundary identity follows cell identity and ring order; topology edits need identity migration.
  • North assumed up (no north arrow observed on plan).
  • Interior door heights (Dtop, DoorA, DoorB, DoorC, DoorD) assumed 2.1m; no interior elevation was supplied for these.
  • Door leaf/swing direction not modeled; only jamb-to-jamb opening width used.
  • EW1 and EW2 (east-wall small windows, L1 and NE_small_room) use the same 1.0-2.8m height as the matching west/south small windows by analogy; East_view.png's own dimension chain only labels the door and the large window, not these small squares, so this height is an assumption, not a direct East_view reading.
