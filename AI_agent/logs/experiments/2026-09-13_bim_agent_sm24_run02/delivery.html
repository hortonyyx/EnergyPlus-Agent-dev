模型采用的假设
  • Correction cell rings represent physical source spaces with horizontal floor/ceiling boundaries.
  • Contacts describe adjacency, not traversability or EnergyPlus surface pairing.
  • Only explicit openings are represented; absent observations do not establish a solid wall.
  • Source boundary identity follows cell identity and ring order; topology edits need identity migration.
  • North=plan top, South=plan bottom, West=plan left, East=plan right, confirmed by matching dimension chains across all 4 elevations and the plan.
  • Exact interior door positions along corridor walls approximated at room-segment midpoints from visible arcs; not pixel-precise.
  • Wall thickness taken as nominal 240mm per plan label, not explicitly modeled as offset per wall (representative centerline geometry used).
  • Corridor modeled as one L-shaped polygon absorbing a jog area near the East wall that contains a secondary exterior door.
  • Window/door head heights inferred from elevation sill/height dimensions (1000mm sill+1800mm or 2400mm height); exact per-window variation not fully verified.
  • Single floor building (only 1F plan supplied); elevations show only ground floor height (4.5m) so treated as single storey.
