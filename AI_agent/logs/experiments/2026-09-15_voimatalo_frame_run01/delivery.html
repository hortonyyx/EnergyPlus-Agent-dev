模型采用的假设
  • Correction cell rings represent physical source spaces with horizontal floor/ceiling boundaries.
  • Contacts describe adjacency, not traversability or EnergyPlus surface pairing.
  • Only explicit openings are represented; absent observations do not establish a solid wall.
  • Source boundary identity follows cell identity and ring order; topology edits need identity migration.
  • Local construction frame is the mesh BIM-local frame rotated yaw=+8 deg about +z to axis-align the building; all coordinates are in this yawed frame (metres).
  • Building is a courtyard/perimeter block: L-shaped 8-storey tall mass (west wing + north/northeast bar) plus a ~2-storey low podium (~6.85m) filling the inner SE corner; far SE is open/not built.
  • Storeys reconciled with declaration: ground (commercial) + six similar office storeys + one setback upper storey = 8; floor-to-floor ~3.4m, ground 4.0m, main roof ~28m.
  • All interior partitions, corridors, cores and doors are explicit hypotheses; no interior evidence exists in the mesh.
  • Window rows are representative regular rhythms preserving piers between openings; exact counts/positions per bay are not surveyed.
  • Rooftop penthouse footprint/height approximate from mesh green roof structures (~31-34m).
