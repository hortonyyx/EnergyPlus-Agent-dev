模型采用的假设
  • Correction cell rings represent physical source spaces with horizontal floor/ceiling boundaries.
  • Contacts describe adjacency, not traversability or EnergyPlus surface pairing.
  • Only explicit openings are represented; absent observations do not establish a solid wall.
  • Source boundary identity follows cell identity and ring order; topology edits need identity migration.
  • Only 4 elevations (S/N/E/W) supplied for a Shenzhen office building, 1F, ~200 sqm; no floor plan was provided.
  • Footprint derived from elevation width labels: South/North elevations both label overall width 10000mm (this is the East-West dimension, x-axis); East/West elevations both label overall width 20000mm (North-South dimension, y-axis). 10m x 20m = 200 sqm, matching stated area.
  • Left/right orientation on each elevation assumed via standard convention (viewer standing outside facing the wall): South image left=West..right=East; North image left=East..right=West; East image left=South..right=North; West image left=North..right=South. Cross-checked: matching 540mm corner margins and coincident window bands between East and West elevations at same y-ranges support this mapping, though it is not independently proven without a plan.
  • No interior partitions, room count, or internal doors are evidenced anywhere in the supplied drawings. Modeled as ONE merged open-plan space (F1_main) covering the full footprint, rather than inventing rooms or splitting by facade orientation.
  • Window/door sill and head heights grouped into 3 repeating dimension patterns observed consistently across facades: standard window (sill 1.0m, height 1.8m), large window >=4000mm wide (sill 1.0m, height 2.4m), exterior door (threshold 0.2m, height 2.4m). Applied uniformly to all openings of matching width/type.
  • Door leaf/swing direction and open/closed state not clearly resolvable from elevations; state left as 'unknown'.
  • Wall thickness abstracted to zero (single representative plane per facade); no interior structural grid assumed.
  • Ceiling height modeled as the full 4.5m elevation height (single storey, no separate slab/plenum breakdown given).
