模型采用的假设
  • Correction cell rings represent physical source spaces with horizontal floor/ceiling boundaries.
  • Contacts describe adjacency, not traversability or EnergyPlus surface pairing.
  • Only explicit openings are represented; absent observations do not establish a solid wall.
  • Source boundary identity follows cell identity and ring order; topology edits need identity migration.
  • Wall representative plane = measured pixel line centrelines/outer faces from pixel_profile scans, not nominal 240mm offsets.
  • Window/door heights: North and East windows sill1.0/head3.4m (directly measured from North_view.png and East_view.png in this review). South and West windows sill1.0/head2.8m (directly measured from South_view.png and West_view.png in this review; West value corrects an inherited unverified assumption that had copied the North pattern).
  • All doors assumed 2.4m height, base 0 (floor level); sub-dimensions near doors in elevations were not fully reconciled and remain an assumption.
  • Building height 4.5m used uniformly as the single floor's wall height, confirmed identical (4500mm) on all four elevations in this review.
  • The corridor (north-south spine) and the south-east furniture area merge into ONE physical room: re-verified in this review by a zoomed crop at [440,580,615,890] -- the partial wall/door near x458-611,y698 does not reach the west corridor edge as a full-height partition, so it cannot enclose a separate room; modeled as open.
  • The full-width north strip (Lobby, x248-611,y151-296) re-checked in this review at [380,145,430,300]: no vertical wall exists between x=400 and the north wall above y=296, confirming Lobby is one open room across the full width.
  • The former ED1_door was reclassified as windows EW0a/EW0b based on its measured 1000mm sill height in the East elevation; this is a substantive correction to the inherited draft, made in this review.
  • EW0a/EW0b (at P5_e2_e3) and EW1a/EW1b (at P6b_e3_se) were each split into two adjacent window openings purely to satisfy the compiler's single-host-per-opening rule where a measured elevation window's world-coordinate span crosses an interior partition's meeting point with the exterior wall; in both cases the East elevation showed one continuous glazed rectangle with no visible mullion at the split point, so whether the real fenestration is one wide unit or two abutting units is NOT confirmed.
  • Room roles (reception, office, boardroom, meeting, pantry, corridor/open office) are inferred from furniture symbols, not labelled in the source.
  • Plan spaces were compiled deterministically from the declared footprint and physical partition segments; no snapping, extension, clipping, or inferred partition was applied.
