  • Interior door heights (all corridor-to-room doors) are assumed at 2.1m, not read from any elevation.
  • Exterior entrance/corridor door heights (2.7m) are a visual-proportion estimate from South_view.png/West_view.png, not a printed dimension label.
  • Pixel-to-world calibration for both floor plans and all four elevations was performed by this agent from the plans' own dimension annotations and has not been independently cross-verified by a second method.
  • claim_0001：South_view.png right-side dimension chain for the small closet window read differently (600/900/1500); this claim applies to the main 2400mm-wide window family (WN1-3, WS1-2) and the similarly-sized east corridor window, not any small closet vent window which is not modeled in this candidate.
模型采用的假设
  • Correction cell rings represent physical source spaces with horizontal floor/ceiling boundaries.
  • Contacts describe adjacency, not traversability or EnergyPlus surface pairing.
  • Only explicit openings are represented; absent observations do not establish a solid wall.
  • Source boundary identity follows cell identity and ring order; topology edits need identity migration.
  • Wall representative plane: single centerline stroke at the measured ink row for each wall, not separate inner/outer faces; footprint uses outer wall faces.
  • F1 exterior window heights (WN1-3, WS1-2, W_east_window) confirmed via North/South/East_view.png dimension chain: sill 1.0m, head 2.6m above the F1 floor (claim_0001, confirmed).
  • F2 exterior window heights (WN1-2, WS_o1-4, W_east_window, W_west_window) confirmed via North/South/East/West_view.png dimension chain: sill 1.0m, head 2.8m above the F2 floor at z_floor=3.0 (claim_0002, confirmed).
  • F1 storey height 3.0m and F2 storey height 3.6m taken directly from the 6600mm total building-height elevation chain (3000+3600).
  • Exterior door heights (F1 south entrance and west corridor door) estimated at 2.7m from visual proportion in South_view.png/West_view.png (door rectangle nearly spans the full 3.0m storey); not from an explicit printed dimension label, so remains a visual estimate rather than a measured chain.
  • All interior corridor-to-room doors (F1 and F2) use an assumed standard height of 2.1m above their floor; no elevation shows interior door heights, so these remain explicit assumptions.
  • North/south exterior window horizontal spans were cross-checked against each elevation's own horizontal dimension chain and matched the plan-derived pixel spans within about 0.1m.
  • A small south-facing ventilation window implied by South_view.png's differently-worded right-side dimension chain (600/900/1500) for the F1 closet area was not modeled; the plan drawing's own dimension chain did not mark a separate window opening at that location, so it is left as an unresolved possible omission.
