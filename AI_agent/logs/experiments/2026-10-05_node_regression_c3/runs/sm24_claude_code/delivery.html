模型采用的假设
  • Correction cell rings represent physical source spaces with horizontal floor/ceiling boundaries.
  • Contacts describe adjacency, not traversability or EnergyPlus surface pairing.
  • Only explicit openings are represented; absent observations do not establish a solid wall.
  • Source boundary identity follows cell identity and ring order; topology edits need identity migration.
  • Interior door heights assumed 0-2.1 m; no interior elevation was supplied.
  • Interior partitions declared on measured line-pair midplanes; drawn 240 mm thicknesses not modelled.
  • Room uses inferred from furniture; no printed use labels on the plan.
  • Plan spaces were compiled deterministically from the declared footprint and physical partition segments; no snapping, extension, clipping, or inferred partition was applied.
