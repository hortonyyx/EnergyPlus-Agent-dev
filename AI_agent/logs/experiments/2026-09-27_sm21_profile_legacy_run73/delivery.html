模型采用的假设
  • Correction cell rings represent physical source spaces with horizontal floor/ceiling boundaries.
  • Contacts describe adjacency, not traversability or EnergyPlus surface pairing.
  • Only explicit openings are represented; absent observations do not establish a solid wall.
  • Source boundary identity follows cell identity and ring order; topology edits need identity migration.
  • ceiling_height: F1=3.0m, F2=3.6m, absolute total 6.6m, all measured from North/South/East/West elevation outer dimension chains (3600/3000/6600)
  • Door heights: F1:D_W_main measured ~2.13m from West_view.png pixels; F1:D_ext_S set to 2.1m by visual-proportion match to the measured main door; all remaining doors (all interior partition doors on F1 and F2, none visible in any elevation) assumed 2.1m standard height, consistent with the measured value but not independently confirmed per-door
  • Window sill/head heights measured from North/East/South_view.png dimension chains per floor and facade family (F1 offices 1.0-2.6m, F1 corridor 1.0-2.8m, F1 narrow accent window 1.5-2.4m, all F2 windows 4.0-5.8m absolute i.e. 1.0-2.8m floor-relative); South/West facades assumed to share the measured North/East chains by symmetry where not independently re-measured
  • Plan spaces were compiled deterministically from the declared footprint and physical partition segments; no snapping, extension, clipping, or inferred partition was applied
  • No stairs/vertical circulation observed in either plan view; the two floors are modeled as independent stacked volumes with no vertical connection
