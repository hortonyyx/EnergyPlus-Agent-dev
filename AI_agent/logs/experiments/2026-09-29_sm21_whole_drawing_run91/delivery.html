  • Calibration (pixel-to-metre anchors) for both floor plans and all four elevations is caller-supplied from measured outer wall faces and dimension-chain pixel positions; it was cross-checked against multiple independent dimension chains (footprint totals, window/door families) with consistent results within about 0.3%, but was not independently re-verified by a second reviewer.
  • Furniture-implied room uses (office/lobby/meeting) are inferred from plan furniture symbols, not from any text label in the drawing.
模型采用的假设
  • Correction cell rings represent physical source spaces with horizontal floor/ceiling boundaries.
  • Contacts describe adjacency, not traversability or EnergyPlus surface pairing.
  • Only explicit openings are represented; absent observations do not establish a solid wall.
  • Source boundary identity follows cell identity and ring order; topology edits need identity migration.
  • Interior partition wall thickness treated as zero per the geometry contract; centerlines were derived from measured pixel gaps and labeled dimension chains (1F: 250mm labeled corridor walls; ~120mm inferred office dividers making the 15000mm chain sum exactly; 2F: clean round-number centerlines at 3.0/5.0m corridor bounds and 3.75/7.5/11.25m office dividers, matching measured pixel gaps within a few percent).
  • Interior corridor-to-office/meeting-room door widths and exact positions (F1:D_N1-3, D_S1-3; F2:D_R1-2, D_O1-4) are approximated from visible arc/frame symbols near the bay-dividing partitions; exact widths are not individually dimensioned in the drawing. Their head heights (2.1m above each floor) are an unobserved standard-door assumption.
  • East-facade window heights (F1:W_E1, F2:EW1) and the corridor's West-facade 2F window (F2:WW1) are assumed to match the North/South window family for their floor (1F sill1.0/head2.6m from claim_0001; 2F sill4.0/head5.8m from claim_0002) by symmetry of the East_view.png/West_view.png dimension pattern (3400/1200/3400), but were not independently re-derived from a dedicated East/West dimension_chain claim in this run.
  • South wall 2F window positions (F2:SW1a/b, SW2a/b) are taken directly from South_view.png's own upper-zone dimension chain (1950/3600/3900/3600/1950=15000mm), which places two 3600mm-wide window bands independent of the 3.75m office grid; each band was split at the office/office partition it crosses so every opening hosts on one wall segment. The correspondence between these two continuous window bands and the four individual offices behind them is a simplification, not an independently confirmed per-office window count.
  • Plan spaces were compiled deterministically from the declared footprint and physical partition segments; no snapping, extension, clipping or inferred partition was applied beyond the stated calibration and centerline choices.
