尚未解决

  • Pixel-to-world calibration for each plan/elevation image was supplied by the agent from the drawings' own dimension chains and outer wall faces; it was cross-checked between plan and elevation (independent chains agree to within ~1%) but not independently verified by a third source.
  • F1:EXT-S-WIN1 (room_wc) and F1 corridor E/W windows use dimension chains adjacent to, but not unambiguously labelled as, those specific openings; assignment is by position and is the most consistent reading found, but remains a judgment call rather than a directly labelled chain.
  • claim_0002：The chain is positioned between the door and this window in the drawing; assignment to the window (rather than a currently-unmodelled feature of the door) is a judgment call based on the window's visibly smaller box matching a 900mm-tall opening.
  • claim_0005：No printed dimension labels the door height directly; the value rests on matching the drawn frame's pixel extent against the calibrated roof/ground/floor-line anchors, not a labelled chain.
模型采用的假设
  • Correction cell rings represent physical source spaces with horizontal floor/ceiling boundaries.
  • Contacts describe adjacency, not traversability or EnergyPlus surface pairing.
  • Only explicit openings are represented; absent observations do not establish a solid wall.
  • Source boundary identity follows cell identity and ring order; topology edits need identity migration.
  • Floor-to-floor heights: 1F ceiling_height=3.0m, 2F z_floor=3.0m/ceiling_height=3.6m, taken from the North/South elevation overall 6600mm dimension chain (3000mm lower band + 3600mm upper band), confirmed by calibrated elevation overlays (roof/ground/floor-line pixel positions matched the chain to within rounding).
  • Plan spaces were compiled deterministically from the declared footprint and physical partition segments; no snapping, extension, clipping, or inferred partition was applied.
  • Interior door heights (all F1 D-N/D-S and F2 D-N/D-S doors) are assumed 2.1m: no plan or elevation drawing dimensions an interior door height.
