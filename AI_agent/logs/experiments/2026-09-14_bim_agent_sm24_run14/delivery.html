模型采用的假设
  • Correction cell rings represent physical source spaces with horizontal floor/ceiling boundaries.
  • Contacts describe adjacency, not traversability or EnergyPlus surface pairing.
  • Only explicit openings are represented; absent observations do not establish a solid wall.
  • Source boundary identity follows cell identity and ring order; topology edits need identity migration.
  • Single-story building (only 1F plan supplied); ceiling height 4.5m taken from facade total height dimension.
  • North=top, South=bottom, West=left, East=right of 1f_view.png, confirmed by matching elevation dimension chains to plan wall dimension chains.
  • Room columns (west/east) and row boundaries derived from coarse dimension chains (4060/2940/4940/8060 west, 4060/1940/5940/3120/4940 east) matched against visible partition lines.
  • Central corridor width taken as gap between visible parallel wall lines (~1.64m), connecting all rooms via doors seen as arcs in plan.
  • Interior door widths/positions are approximate (visible arcs used for host wall and general position, but not measured precisely); D5b (corridor-west_storage2) is inferred since only one clear arc was seen serving the double storage room area.
  • Window sill/head heights approximated from elevation dimension groups (typically sill 1.0m, head 3.4m on N/W/E walls; sill 1.0m head 2.8m on S windows); exact figures not individually re-verified per opening.
  • Door heights set to 2.4m (floor to head) as a standard assumption; some elevation dimension chains showed unusual bottom offsets (e.g. 200mm) not modeled.
  • Wall thickness observed as 240mm in several plan annotations but not implemented as explicit wall_references/offsets in this pass.
